Good questions

Everything guests ask before they say yes.

How do six voices create a full-band sound?+

The performance is built vocally — rhythm, bass, melody, harmony and instrumental effects — supported by theatrical video, lighting and production.

Is SIX a concert or a comedy show?+

It's a two-act vocal-band experience built from music, live sound, family chemistry, heart and naturally occurring brother comedy.

Is the show a good fit for different generations?+

The musical range and family connection are designed to give multiple generations something to recognize, enjoy and talk about afterward.

When does SIX perform?+

The typical pattern is evenings Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 8 PM, matinees Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 3 PM, dark on Sundays. Live dates will always come from the approved ticket calendar.

Where does SIX perform?+

SIX performs at Pepsi Legends Theater, 1600 W 76 Country Blvd in Branson, Missouri.
